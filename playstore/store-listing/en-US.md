# OhmLauncher

## Short description

An Omarchy-inspired home screen with themes, widgets, and fast app search.

## Full description

OhmLauncher brings the focused look and feel of Omarchy to your Android home screen. Start in Omarchy mode, then make it yours with bundled themes and backgrounds.

Make your home screen work your way

• Keep favorite apps close and find any installed app with fast search.
• Organize apps and shortcuts in configurable edge boxes.
• Add Android widgets and local plugins to build a layout that fits your routine.
• Open the Quake-style terminal whenever you want quick access to supported commands.
• Bring your screen to life with animated TTFX backgrounds and optional audio-reactive visuals.

Your launcher setup stays on your phone. If you choose to pair an Omarchy computer, themes and backgrounds sync directly over your local network.

To use OhmLauncher as your home screen, choose it as the default launcher in Android settings. You can change your default home app at any time.
